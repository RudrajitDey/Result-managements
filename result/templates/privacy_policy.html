Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 02 February 2026

Result Center Management System ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use our system.

1. Information We Collect

  • Student Information: Name, roll number, class, section, and academic details.
  • Result Data: Marks, grades, attendance records, and performance reports.
  • User Login Data: Username, password (encrypted), role (admin/teacher/student).
  • System Logs: Login time, actions performed (for security and auditing).

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To manage student results and academic records.
  • To generate report cards, mark sheets, and performance analytics.
  • To provide secure login access for authorized users.
  • To maintain system security and prevent unauthorized access.

3. Data Security

We use standard security measures such as password encryption, role-based access, and secure storage to protect your data. However, no system is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee complete protection against cyber threats.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent user data. Information may be shared only in these cases:

  • With school/college authorities for official academic purposes.
  • When required by law or government regulations.
  • To protect the safety and integrity of the system.

5. Cookies & Tracking

Our system may use cookies or session storage to maintain login sessions and improve user experience. We do not use cookies for advertising.

6. Data Retention

We store student results and records as long as required for academic purposes. Data may be deleted or archived after the completion of the academic session or as per institutional policy.

7. User Rights

  • Users can request correction of incorrect student data.
  • Users can request deletion of data (if allowed by institution rules).
  • Users can reset passwords and update account details securely.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with the latest date.

Contact Us:
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the system administrator or your institution management.
