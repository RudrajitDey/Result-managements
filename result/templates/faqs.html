What is Student Result Management System? ▼
Student Result Management System is a comprehensive digital platform designed to help educational institutions efficiently manage, track, and analyze student academic results. It provides tools for result entry, storage, analysis, reporting, and communication with students and parents.
Who can use this system? ▼
Our system is designed for educational institutions of all sizes, including schools, colleges, universities, tutoring centers, and training institutes. It serves administrators, teachers, students, and parents.
How quickly can I get started? ▼
You can get started within minutes! Our streamlined onboarding process allows you to set up your institution, add students, and begin managing results immediately. We also provide comprehensive documentation and video tutorials.
What pricing plans are available? ▼
We offer three pricing plans: Basic ($29/month for up to 500 students), Professional ($79/month for growing institutions), and Enterprise (custom pricing for large institutions). Each plan includes different features and support levels.
Can I change my plan later? ▼
Yes! You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Changes take effect at the beginning of your next billing cycle, and we'll prorate any differences.
Is there a free trial available? ▼
Yes! We offer a 14-day free trial with full access to all Professional plan features. No credit card required to start your trial.
What are the system requirements? ▼
Our system is web-based and works on any modern browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge) on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices. No software installation required. An internet connection is needed for access.
Can I import existing data? ▼
Yes! We support data import from Excel, CSV, and other common formats. Our migration team can also assist with transferring data from your existing system.
Is there an API available? ▼
Yes! Enterprise plan includes full API access for custom integrations. Professional plan offers limited API access for basic operations.
How secure is my data? ▼
We use enterprise-grade security measures including SSL encryption, regular security audits, and compliance with data protection regulations like GDPR and FERPA. Your data is backed up regularly and stored in secure, redundant servers.
Where is my data stored? ▼
Your data is stored in secure, SOC 2 compliant data centers with redundant backups. We offer data residency options for specific regions to comply with local regulations.
What kind of support do you offer? ▼
We offer 24/7 customer support through email, phone, and live chat. Basic plan includes email support, Professional includes phone and live chat, Enterprise includes priority support with dedicated account managers.
Do you provide training? ▼
Yes! We provide comprehensive training including video tutorials, documentation, webinars, and personalized training sessions for Enterprise customers.
