Help & Support

Welcome to the Help Center of Result Center Management System. Here you can find quick guidance on using the system, solving common issues, and contacting support.

1. Quick Guide

  • Students: Login → View Results → Download/Print Marksheet
  • Teachers: Login → Enter Marks → Save/Update → Publish Results
  • Admin: Login → Manage Users → Manage Classes/Subjects → Generate Reports

2. Common Issues & Solutions

  • Cannot login: Check username/password or reset password.
  • Result not showing: Results may not be published yet by admin/teacher.
  • Wrong marks: Contact class teacher or system admin for correction.
  • Page not loading: Refresh browser or check internet connection.

3. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How can I reset my password?

Click on "Forgot Password" on the login page and follow the instructions. If you don’t have access, contact your admin.

How do I download my marksheet?

Login → Go to Results → Select Exam → Click Download/Print.

Who can edit marks after submission?

Only authorized staff (teacher/admin) can edit marks. Students cannot edit results.

Why is my result showing "Not Published"?

Your teacher/admin has not published the result yet. Please wait or contact the institution.

How to report a technical issue?

You can report issues using the contact details below. Mention your username, class, and problem screenshot if possible.
