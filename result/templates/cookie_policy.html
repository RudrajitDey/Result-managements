Cookie Policy

Last Updated: 02 February 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how the Result Center Management System ("we", "our", "us") uses cookies and similar technologies to provide secure login, improve performance, and enhance your experience.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device (computer/mobile) when you visit a website. They help the website remember your preferences and maintain sessions.

2. Why We Use Cookies

  • To keep you logged in securely (session cookies).
  • To remember user preferences and settings.
  • To protect the system from unauthorized access.
  • To improve system performance and user experience.

3. Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential Cookies: Required for login, authentication, and security.
  • Functional Cookies: Helps remember user preferences (example: theme, language).
  • Performance Cookies: Helps analyze system usage to improve speed and reliability.

4. Third-Party Cookies

We do not use advertising cookies. If any third-party service is integrated (such as analytics or messaging), it may use cookies as per their policies.

5. How You Can Control Cookies

You can control or delete cookies from your browser settings. However, disabling essential cookies may affect login and system functionality.

6. Updates to This Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy when needed. Any changes will be posted on this page with the updated date.

7. Contact

If you have any questions about our Cookie Policy, please contact the system administrator or institution management.
