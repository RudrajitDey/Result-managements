Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly can I get started with the system? ▼
You can get started within minutes! Our streamlined onboarding process allows you to set up your institution, add students, and begin managing results immediately. We also provide comprehensive documentation and video tutorials to help you get the most out of our platform.
What kind of support do you offer? ▼
We offer 24/7 customer support through email, phone, and live chat. Our dedicated support team is always ready to help you with any questions or issues you may encounter. Premium and Enterprise packages also include priority support with dedicated account managers.
Is my data secure? ▼
Absolutely! We use enterprise-grade security measures including SSL encryption, regular security audits, and compliance with data protection regulations like GDPR and FERPA. Your data is backed up regularly and stored in secure, redundant servers.
Can I customize the system for my institution? ▼
Yes! Our Enterprise package offers full customization options including custom branding, integrations with your existing systems, and tailored features to meet your specific needs. We also offer API access for advanced customizations.
